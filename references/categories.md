# Category playbooks

Visual codes per category: what the audience expects (use it for trust) and where to break it (for distinctiveness).
Each entry: **job of the post · codes · layouts · type · watch-outs**.

## Real estate / property advisory
- Job: trust + aspiration + clarity. Buyers want location logic, proof and calm.
- Codes: daylight architecture, aerials with plot outlines and pins (R04), airy white/sky fields (R01), navy+gold for
  launches/events (R03), landscape-led second homes (R15), lifestyle aspiration (R17).
- Layouts: Dissolve, Split+panorama, Explainer with boxed keywords (R14), illustrated comic for legal/process topics (R16).
- Type: calm sans (two-tier headline) or elegant thin serif + sans.
- Watch: RERA/regulatory IDs and "representative image" disclaimers where needed; never invent prices, returns or
  approvals; AI renders must not imply a real project looks like that unless it's the developer's render.

## Food & beverage / cafés / restaurants
- Job: appetite + personality + "where/when".
- Codes: flat colour backdrops, macro texture, top-down and 3/4 hero shots, witty dish names in display serif (R10),
  seasonal colour worlds (R12), ingredient→dish strips (R13).
- Layouts: Hero ↔ macro rhythm, monochrome world, panorama strip, menu card, event zine for nights/festivals (R11).
- Watch: food must be real photography of the actual dish when possible; price and availability (outlets, dates) exact.

## Fashion, beauty, lifestyle
- Job: desire + identity. Editorial restraint wins.
- Codes: big imagery, small type, magazine grammar (cover lines, page numbers), cut-outs, inset frames.
- Layouts: full-bleed with tiny caption, editorial grid, lookbook pairs, product hero on colour.
- Watch: skin tones accurate; no over-retouch; photo prompts via `ugc-fashion-shoot-pro` / `premium-urban-shoot`.

## Events, music, festivals, nightlife
- Job: FOMO + information (what, where, when, tickets).
- Codes: zine/poster, stickers, tickets, mono info lists, countdown numerals, saturated flats, grain (R11).
- Layouts: Zine, poster, lineup grid, countdown, four-corner for artists/speakers (R07).
- Watch: date/time/venue legible at phone size; one ticket CTA.

## Film, media, culture, agencies
- Job: prestige + curiosity.
- Codes: one-sheet grammar (credits row, letter-spaced title, billing block), laurels and pull-quotes (R08),
  documentary photo series with whisper-quiet type (R06), dark studio glow (R05).
- Watch: credits accurate; quotes must be real and attributed.

## B2B, SaaS, consulting, finance
- Job: authority + usefulness (saves).
- Codes: explainer system, black/white + one accent, charts, frameworks, formulas, receipts, highlight boxes.
- Layouts: Explainer, Type statement, chart-first, before/after, myth vs fact.
- Watch: numbers sourced; finance needs disclaimers ("not investment advice", regulator rules); no guaranteed returns.

## Creator / personal brand / education
- Job: trust in a person + a takeaway worth saving.
- Codes: face on cover (cut-out or candid), handwritten marks, series numbering, consistent template, Part 1/Part 2.
- Layouts: Type statement with face chip, step lists, comic/illustration series, receipts/screenshots with highlights.
- Watch: the creator's face only from real photos they supplied; keep their template rigid so the grid is recognisable.

## Health, wellness, clinics
- Job: reassurance + clarity.
- Codes: soft naturals, texture, calm serif + humanist sans, simple diagrams.
- Watch: no medical claims without source; before/after rules per platform and law; sensitive topics → illustration over stock faces.

## Education / institutes / ed-tech
- Job: outcome + credibility.
- Codes: student outcomes, campus life, bright but systemised palettes, numbers as heroes.
- Watch: real results only; avoid cluttered "admissions open" posters: one message, one CTA.

## D2C products / retail
- Job: desire + reason to buy now.
- Codes: product hero on colour, macro of material, benefit callouts with thin leader lines, offer badge (one), UGC proof.
- Watch: price/offer terms exact; avoid "SALE!!!" clutter; the product must be the real one.

## Manufacturing / industrial / exports
- Job: capability + trust (buyers, partners).
- Codes: process photography, precision details, specs as typography, certifications row, dark steel or clean white systems.
- Watch: certifications and partner logos only if real and permitted.

## Festivals and greetings (any brand)
- Job: warmth + brand relevance. Tie the festival to the brand's world (a café's Diwali = sweets on their counter;
  a real-estate firm's Diwali = lights in a home they built), not generic stock diyas/fireworks.
- Keep the logo small; the greeting short; one visual idea.

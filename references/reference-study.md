# Reference study: 17 brand posts, slide by slide

## 1. Post by post

### R01 Guardians: "Is the next generation changing the address game?" (6 slides, 4:5)
- **Template, every slide identical**: logo top-left (small, ~4% of width), headline centred upper third,
  photoreal architecture image fills the lower 2/3 and *dissolves upward into a white-to-sky gradient*,
  so text sits on calm air, never on busy pixels.
- **Two-tier headline**: line 1 small caps, regular weight, mauve-grey ("IS THE NEXT GENERATION CHANGING");
  line 2 heavy *italic* caps, darker ("THE ADDRESS GAME?"). Weight + style contrast does the emphasis, no colour needed.
- Sub-line: one sentence, small grey sans, centred.
- **Takeaway pill** at the bottom of each slide: dark translucent rounded bar, white text, one-sentence
  moral ("Convenience influences desirability."). Every slide closes on a quotable line.
- Cover: `>>` swipe chevrons bottom-right. Narrative: question → 4 shifts → "they're choosing how to live" payoff.
- Palette: white, sky-blue haze, mauve-grey text, warm daylight photography. Calm, premium, advisory.

### R02 Guardians: "What makes a project age well?" (6 slides)
- Same template as R01 (series consistency = brand recognition in the grid).
- Cover image has a **diagonal white slash** splitting two building photos (before/after feel).
- Pill moves directly under the sub-line when the image is busy at the bottom.
- Last slide is a **formula**: "Planning + Design + Usability + Maintenance + Ecosystem" (summary as an equation, very saveable).

### R03 Guardians × Right Channel: "THANK YOU!" event post (single, 4:5)
- Deep navy field, thin gold inset border frame, gold metallic serif caps headline with soft glow.
- 2-2-2 photo grid with rounded corners (event proof), gold silk swoosh bottom-left as the only ornament.
- Co-brand lockup top-left with a hairline divider; compliance top-right (RERA number + QR).
- Project logo lockup centred at the bottom, disclaimer micro-type footer.
- Lesson: luxury = dark field + one metal + thin rules + generous margins; compliance can be elegant if it is aligned and small.

### R04 Guardians: land-deal announcement (single, 4:5)
- Aerial AI coastal city; the land parcel outlined with a **dotted line + map pin** (the subject is drawn on the photo).
- Frosted white glass card holds three partner logos; brand logo white top-right. Almost no words; logos are the message.
- "Representative image" micro-disclaimer bottom-right.

### R05 Owled Media: "A video content studio built for the future" (6 slides, mixed image + video)
- Dark plum-black gradient with faint vertical pinstripes, glowing amber retro TVs as the hero object.
- Headline: big *italic serif* in warm cream/yellow ("We do this end to end."); body white sans; **bold on key words**
  ("script to final video", "TV, OTT, IPL").
- Big stat in yellow ("500+ videos"). Services as a centred list. Client logo wall on the proof slide.
- **Seamless panorama**: a photo and a film strip cross the slide boundary, so the swipe feels continuous.
- Small `→` arrow at the bottom of each slide as the swipe cue.

### R06 Talented × Forest Essentials: "Project Paathshala" (10 slides, landscape)
- Documentary photography, warm, shallow depth; red school uniforms repeat as a **colour motif** across all 10 frames.
- Text only on the cover: small letter-spaced gold serif caps title placed in the dark negative space,
  a tiny italic stat stack top-right ("10 years / 167 partner schools / 2,000 children"), tagline micro-line.
- Lesson: when the photographs are strong, type should whisper. Restraint reads as confidence.

### R07 Good Ads Matter: speaker card (single, 4:5)
- B&W portrait fills the frame, bottom fades to black for legibility.
- **Four-corner information layout**: event logo top-left, venue-partner top-right, role mid-left (white),
  name mid-right (gold/yellow caps), date bottom-left, venue bottom-right. Face owns the centre.
- One accent colour (gold) for the name and logo only. Clean geometric sans throughout.

### R08 Talented: "LAPARVAHI" short-film carousel (3 slides, 4:5)
- Classic one-sheet grammar: cast names row in tiny caps across the top, widely letter-spaced gold serif title
  + Devanagari title under it, condensed billing block at the bottom, production logos in the corners.
- Slides 2–3: **pull-quote testimonials** (italic serif in warm gold, name in small caps + title) placed in
  the dark negative space of film stills. Social proof styled like festival laurels, not like reviews.
- Warm teal-amber grade on every frame.

### R10 Katha Coffee: "Rise & Dine" breakfast menu (10 slides, 4:5)
- **Hero/detail rhythm**: product hero on a flat deep-green or brown backdrop with the dish name in warm mustard
  serif (mix of italic and roman, e.g. "*Diner* pancake", "*Not a* Kaya toast") → next slide a full-bleed macro
  close-up of the same dish with no text → next hero. Appetite is built by the macro, information by the hero.
- Witty dish names *are* the copy. Tiny brand flower icon in a corner as the only branding.
- Video slides open and close the carousel (bookends).

### R11 Katha Crafthouse: "AM-PM Festival" (6 slides, 4:5)
- **Zine / event-poster system**: spiral-binder rings down the left edge (notebook metaphor), each slide a
  different saturated flat colour (navy, forest, red, pink, navy, mustard), yet one system:
  - giant rotated "AM" / "PM" wordmark bleeding off the right edge,
  - photos inside **ticket-stub frames** (notched corners),
  - retro **sticker badges** (MELA, LIFE, PICNIC, KILL THE KEG) overlapping frames at angles,
  - info lists in typewriter/mono caps, chunky 70s display face for headlines,
  - countdown slide: "2 | 6 — DAYS | WORLDS" as big numerals split by a rule, "LIMITED TIX ONLY" ticket badge.
- Paper grain on every slide. Lesson: you can change colour every slide if the *grid, type and motifs* stay fixed.

### R12 Katha: "Project Garmi" mango desserts (4 slides)
- **Monochrome colour world**: orange product on orange paper on an orange backdrop with faint sun-ray motif.
- White serif product names (italic + roman mix), one small body line, sub-brand lockup "PROJECT गर्मी"
  mixing Latin serif with a red Devanagari stamp. Bilingual type as identity.

### R13 True Black: "Red Pepper Hummus Toast" (3 slides = one panorama)
- Cream field, black high-contrast serif caps title, `→`, a single line icon (bread) on slide 1.
- An **ingredient → dish strip** runs across the slide seams: hummus texture | chickpeas + peppers | finished toast.
- Small caps caption under the strip. Editorial, gallery-like, lots of empty cream.

### R14 Lokations: "What exactly is an Aerotropolis?" (5 slides)
- Explainer system: slides alternate **white / black** backgrounds; geometric sans (Montserrat-like) throughout.
- **Highlight-box keyword**: one or two words per slide sit in an inverted box ("city", "more than",
  "investing", "15 lakh sq. ft."). The eye reads the boxed words first and gets the story.
- Line-icon rows separated by vertical hairlines (Offices | Hotels | Shopping | Homes | Business parks).
- Cover: 3×2 photo grid + question headline with the key term boxed. Last slide: "Follow [Lokations]" CTA.

### R15 Lokations: "Second home" (5 slides)
- Cover and close: full-bleed forest photo in a **white inset frame**, elegant thin serif "Second home." + a tiny
  "You need a clear plan. ⟶" pill as the swipe cue.
- Middle 3 slides: top half white with heavy condensed caps headline + three options split by vertical rules;
  bottom half a **landscape panorama that continues across all three slides** (lake → bench → house).
- Close: "The plan is simple: Budget + Location + Check + Buy" formula, brand name in large serif.

### R16 Lokations: "5 signs the land title is fake — Part 1" (8 slides)
- **Illustrated comic** with one recurring character (man in a blue check shirt), warm painterly cartoon style.
- Headline top-left, sentence case, **bold only on the punch words** ("The owner's story keeps **changing**").
- Short body (2–3 lines), illustration fills the lower 60%.
- Cover: hand-lettered title + yellow "Part 1" pill. Close: "Comment **Lokations** for Part 2" (comment-keyword CTA,
  series hook → comments and follows).

### R17 Lokations: "How billionaires choose real estate" (6 slides)
- Aspirational full-bleed cinematic photography (vintage car, lake, coffee cherries, tea hills, couple on deck).
- Mixed-weight headline: "How **BILLIONAIRES** choose real estate" (one word huge).
- Bottom-left statement in bold white + small body. Last slide: teaser "That's a very different way to look at…
  REAL ESTATE".
- Weakness: small white body text sits on bright busy photos in places; legibility suffers. A scrim or shadow was needed.

## 2. Patterns across the set (what the pros keep doing)

1. **One template per series.** Guardians and Lokations reuse a fixed grid so a grid of their posts reads as one brand.
2. **Text on calm, image on busy.** Gradients, scrims, inset panels, frosted cards, dark negative space; the type never
   fights the photo (the only failure seen, R17, is where this was skipped).
3. **Emphasis by contrast of weight/style, not by more colours**: small regular line + heavy italic line; bold punch word;
   one boxed word; one gold name.
4. **One accent colour.** Gold on navy/black, mustard on green, yellow on plum, red stamp on orange.
5. **Every slide ends on a quotable line** (pills, formulas, takeaways) → saves and shares.
6. **Covers are questions or promises** ("What makes a project age well?", "5 signs…", "How billionaires…").
7. **Swipe cues**: `>>`, `→`, pill with arrow, cut-off image or panorama that crosses the seam.
8. **Seamless panoramas** (Owled, True Black, Lokations) make the carousel feel like one object.
9. **Rhythm**: hero ↔ detail (Katha), white ↔ black (Lokations), image ↔ type.
10. **Recurring motif**: red uniforms, binder rings, AM/PM wordmark, sun rays, one cartoon character.
11. **Close with a reason to act**: "Follow", "Comment X for Part 2", "Limited tix only", formula recap.
12. **Brand marks stay small** (logo ~4–8% width, corner) — the idea is the hero, not the logo.
13. **Category codes**: real estate = airy daylight, AI-rendered architecture, calm sans; F&B = flat colour backdrops,
    macro texture, witty serif names; film/culture = one-sheet grammar, letter-spaced serif, billing blocks;
    events = zine, stickers, tickets, mono info lists; corporate studio = dark gradient, glow, italic serif.
